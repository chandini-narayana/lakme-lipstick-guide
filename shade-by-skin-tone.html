Student project notice: this site is an academic demonstration built to practise Google Search Console. It is not operated by, or affiliated with, Lakmé or Hindustan Unilever. Product facts are taken from the official Lakmé India store and may change.

Lakmé's product pages group shades into Pinks, Reds, Nudes and Browns and offer a virtual Try On feature. A practical shortlist method:

  1. Check your undertone (warm, cool or neutral) in daylight.
  2. Pick a shade family for the occasion: Nudes for daily wear, Reds for events, Pinks for a fresh look.
  3. Use the official Try On tool, then compare two neighbouring shades.
