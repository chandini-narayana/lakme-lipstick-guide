About this project

Student project notice: this site is an academic demonstration built to practise Google Search Console. It is not operated by, or affiliated with, Lakmé or Hindustan Unilever. Product facts are taken from the official Lakmé India store and may change.

The site contains six short pages, each with a unique title, meta description, canonical URL and internal links, plus sitemap.xml and robots.txt. It exists to practise Search Console verification, sitemap submission and URL inspection.

Academic demonstration for a Digital Marketing & Automation AAT, Global Academy of Technology. Lakmé is a trademark of its owner; no affiliation or endorsement is claimed.
